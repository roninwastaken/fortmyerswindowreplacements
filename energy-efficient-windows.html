Frequently Asked Questions

How much can energy-efficient windows lower my cooling bill?
Results vary by home, but Low-E glass with insulated frames typically reduces solar heat gain significantly compared to older single-pane or clear dual-pane windows, which can noticeably cut AC run time during Fort Myers’ hottest months.
How much does window replacement cost in Fort Myers?
Standard replacement windows in Fort Myers typically run $450–$1,100 per window installed, depending on size, frame material, and glass package. Impact-rated hurricane windows run higher, generally $750–$1,900 per window, because of the reinforced glass and frame required to meet Florida Building Code wind-load standards.
Do you offer warranties on window replacement?
Yes. Installation workmanship is covered by our labor warranty, and window products carry manufacturer warranties that vary by product line. We review exact warranty terms for your selected windows before you approve any project.
Do I need a permit to replace windows in Lee County?
Most window replacement projects in Lee County and the City of Fort Myers require a building permit, especially for impact-rated windows, since inspectors verify the installation meets Florida Building Code wind-load requirements. We handle permitting and inspection scheduling as part of our installation process.

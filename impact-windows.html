Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between impact windows and hurricane shutters?
Impact windows are permanently installed laminated-glass windows that provide protection and clear views year-round with no setup required. Hurricane shutters are removable or foldable panels you deploy before a storm. Impact windows cost more upfront but add daily UV and noise reduction, security, and typically qualify for larger insurance discounts than shutters alone.
How much do impact windows cost in Fort Myers?
Impact window installation in Fort Myers generally costs $750–$1,900 per window depending on size, frame material, and glass configuration. We provide itemized free estimates before any work begins.
Do impact windows lower my homeowners insurance in Florida?
In many cases, yes. Florida insurers often apply wind-mitigation discounts for homes with impact-rated windows and doors. Ask your insurance agent for a wind-mitigation inspection after installation to confirm your specific discount.
Do I need a permit to replace windows in Lee County?
Most window replacement projects in Lee County and the City of Fort Myers require a building permit, especially for impact-rated windows, since inspectors verify the installation meets Florida Building Code wind-load requirements. We handle permitting and inspection scheduling as part of our installation process.

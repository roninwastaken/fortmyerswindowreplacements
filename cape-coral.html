Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on waterfront and canal-front homes in Cape Coral?
Yes. A large share of our Cape Coral projects are on canal-front or waterfront homes, where large sliding glass doors and picture windows are common. We use frame materials and sealing methods suited to the added salt air and humidity exposure near the water.
How much does window replacement cost?
Standard replacement windows typically run $450–$1,100 per window installed, depending on size, frame material, and glass package. Impact-rated hurricane windows run $750–$1,900 per window because of the reinforced glass and frame required to meet Florida Building Code wind-load standards.
Do I need a permit to replace windows here?
In most cases, yes, especially for impact-rated windows, since inspectors verify the installation meets Florida Building Code wind-load requirements. We handle permitting and inspection scheduling as part of our installation process.

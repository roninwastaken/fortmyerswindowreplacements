Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on homes still being rebuilt after Hurricane Ian?
Yes. We work with homeowners and contractors on Fort Myers Beach at every stage of rebuilding, installing impact windows that meet current Florida Building Code requirements for coastal construction.
Do impact windows lower my homeowners insurance in Florida?
In many cases, yes. Florida insurers often apply wind-mitigation discounts for homes with impact-rated windows and doors. Ask your insurance agent for a wind-mitigation inspection after installation to confirm your specific discount.
Do I need a permit to replace windows here?
In most cases, yes, especially for impact-rated windows, since inspectors verify the installation meets Florida Building Code wind-load requirements. We handle permitting and inspection scheduling as part of our installation process.

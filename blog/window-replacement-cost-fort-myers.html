How Much Does Window Replacement Cost in Fort Myers?

A breakdown of real costs — materials, labor, and what affects pricing.

Window replacement pricing in Fort Myers varies more than people expect, mostly because “a window” isn’t one product — it’s a combination of size, frame material, glass type, and code requirements that all move the price independently.

Standard replacement vs. impact-rated windows

  • Standard replacement windows: the more budget-friendly option, with cost driven mainly by size, frame material, and glass package.
  • Impact-rated hurricane windows: a bigger investment than standard replacement, reflecting the laminated glass and reinforced framing required to meet Florida Building Code wind-load standards.

Whole-home projects scale with window count, so a home converting fully to impact glass is a meaningfully bigger investment than a single problem window — but per-window pricing generally improves slightly on larger orders.

What actually drives the price up or down

Window size and configuration

A small bathroom window costs far less than a large picture window or a multi-panel slider. Custom shapes (arches, bay/bow configurations) also add cost over standard rectangular openings.

Frame material

Vinyl is typically the most budget-friendly option for standard replacement. Aluminum frames, often used in impact-rated systems for their strength, run higher. Fiberglass and premium composite frames sit at the top of the range.

Glass package

Standard dual-pane glass costs less than impact-rated laminated glass, and Low-E energy-efficient coatings add a modest premium that typically pays for itself over time in lower cooling bills.

Number of openings and accessibility

Second-story windows, windows behind landscaping or furniture, and homes with unusual access all add modest labor time.

Permitting

Most window replacement in Lee County and the City of Fort Myers requires a permit, especially for impact-rated installs. Permit and inspection coordination is typically built into your quote rather than billed as a hidden add-on.

Getting an accurate number for your home

Because so many variables affect the final price, the only reliable way to budget is a free, itemized, on-site estimate — a per-window ballpark online can be off by hundreds of dollars once your specific frame material, glass package, and window count are factored in.
